What I work on

I mainly work with adults on issues related to work and personal life: a creeping exhaustion, anxiety running in loops, a feeling of no longer fitting in, difficulty setting boundaries. Not necessarily a crisis. Just something weighing on you.

My framework is Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) — a scientifically validated approach that works on the link between what we think, feel, and do.

Burnout Bore-out Anxiety Stress Sense of purpose at work Mental load Self-confidence Emotional regulation Work-life balance
